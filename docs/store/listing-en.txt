Restore It! Jomon & Haniwa

Short description (max 80 characters)
Rebuild shattered Japanese pottery, dogu and haniwa from 3D fragments!

Full description
You are the restorer at an excavation site.
Ancient treasures have been shattered into pieces. Put them back together, one by one.

■ How to play
- Before you start, spin the finished artifact around and take a good look
- Tap "Start" and it shatters into pieces!
- Drag a piece close to the right spot and it snaps into place
- Build from the bottom up, starting with the base and the feet
- Light and sound on every snap. When you finish, the artifact is whole again, with no cracks

■ 9 stages
1 Flame-style pottery (Middle Jomon period)
2 Dogu with goggle-like eyes (Final Jomon period)
3 Jomon Venus (National Treasure)
4 Masked Goddess (National Treasure)
5 "Tora-san" haniwa
6 Horse haniwa
7 Haniwa warrior in armor (National Treasure)
8 Heart-shaped dogu (Important Cultural Property)
9 Haniwa "Dancing People"
Later stages have more pieces, tighter snapping and a fainter guide.
Clear a stage to unlock the next one.

■ Look and learn
- Rotate each 3D artifact with your finger and see it from any angle
- After each clear, read about its era, where it was found and what makes it special
- Calm music and a ticking clock help you focus

■ Also
- Hint (the next piece's spot lights up) and Guide (a faint outline of the finished shape)
- "Auto": watch an ad to snap the next piece into place (up to 5 times per stage)
- Share your result
- Playable offline (ads and sharing need a connection)
- Available in English and Japanese

* The 3D models are based on the features of each artifact and are not exact copies of the real objects.
